# Store Screenshots Checklist

## Primary Screenshot Set

1. **Home**
   - Visible: family name, progress summary, active tasks preview, waiting actions, goal preview, recent events, quick actions.
   - Meaning: one place for the family's day and next actions.
   - Best state: real family with 2-3 members, one active task, one waiting action, active family goal.
   - Best role: owner/adult.

2. **Tasks**
   - Visible: active tasks list, statuses, reward/points, assignee, create-task action.
   - Meaning: chores and responsibilities are clear and trackable.
   - Best state: mix of active task, submitted task waiting review, completed/recent task.
   - Best role: owner/adult for create/review controls; child variant can show assigned tasks.

3. **Family Goal**
   - Visible: goal title, target/progress, contribution action, member contributions or confirmation state.
   - Meaning: family works toward a shared reward together.
   - Best state: active goal at 50-75% progress, with at least two contributors.
   - Best role: family with child; owner/adult if showing create/update controls.

4. **Rewards**
   - Visible: approved rewards, cost/free-level hint if available, request action, request status.
   - Meaning: earned progress turns into meaningful family rewards.
   - Best state: several approved rewards and one active reward request.
   - Best role: child for requesting; owner/adult for approving/fulfilling.

5. **Initiatives**
   - Visible: initiative cards, discussion/waiting decision status, decision actions.
   - Meaning: children and adults can propose ideas and resolve them fairly.
   - Best state: one initiative in discussion, one waiting decision, one resolved recent item.
   - Best role: owner/adult for decision actions; child variant for submitted ideas.

6. **History**
   - Visible: recent family events with dates, event types, filters if present.
   - Meaning: the app keeps a transparent activity trail.
   - Best state: populated timeline from tasks, rewards, initiatives, and goal contributions.
   - Best role: owner/adult.

7. **Rating**
   - Visible: family member rankings, XP/level/progress indicators.
   - Meaning: progress is visible and motivating without opening every feature.
   - Best state: 3-4 members with different levels and XP.
   - Best role: family with child.

8. **Notifications**
   - Visible: unread/read notifications, timestamps, load-more state if useful.
   - Meaning: important family actions do not get lost.
   - Best state: 2-3 unread notifications and a few read ones.
   - Best role: owner/adult or child, depending on the notification content.

## Optional Supporting Screenshots

- **Family setup**
  - Use only if store set needs onboarding context.
  - Best state: join/create family choice or invite-code entry.
  - Best role: new user.

- **Profile / family settings**
  - Use only if showing account/family management is important for beta testers.
  - Best state: populated family members, visible role labels, safe settings actions.
  - Best role: owner.

## Family With Child

- Prioritize these screenshots with a child member in the family:
  - Home.
  - Tasks.
  - Family Goal.
  - Rewards.
  - Rating.
  - Notifications if they reference assigned tasks, approvals, or rewards.

## Owner / Adult Role

- Prioritize these screenshots with owner/adult role:
  - Home with quick actions.
  - Tasks with create/review controls.
  - Initiatives with decision controls.
  - Rewards with approve/fulfill controls.
  - Family Goal with create/update or completion confirmation.
  - Profile / family settings with member roles.
